Our Story

We're on a Mission to Electrify Everything

Born from a simple frustration — unreliable charging — VoltGrid set out to build the infrastructure that the EV revolution actually deserves.

8+
Years of Innovation
Mission Statement

The VoltGrid Vision

In 2018, our founders — a trio of engineers from Tesla, ABB, and Siemens — experienced firsthand the anxiety of a broken charging station during a road trip. That moment of frustration became the seed of VoltGrid.

Our mission is straightforward: make EV charging as reliable and effortless as switching on a light. We believe that the biggest barrier to EV adoption isn't the vehicles — it's the charging infrastructure. So we're fixing it.

Our Journey

From Idea to Global Network

2018
The Spark

Three engineers quit their jobs and incorporated VoltGrid Inc. in San Francisco. First angel round of $2M closed within 3 months.

2019
First 100 Stations

Launched pilot network of 100 DC fast chargers across California. Achieved 99.1% uptime in year one — setting the industry benchmark.

2020
Series A & European Expansion

$45M Series A. Launched in Germany, Netherlands, and Norway. Introduced the VoltGrid app with route planning.

2021
Plug & Charge Pioneer

First US charging network to deploy ISO 15118 Plug & Charge across the entire fleet. Fleet Pro platform launched for commercial operators.

2022
350kW Ultra-Fast Network

$200M Series B. Rolled out the world's first commercial 350kW ultra-fast network. Expanded to 28 countries.

2023
Solar + V2G Integration

Launched solar integration platform and Vehicle-to-Grid capabilities. 500,000 registered drivers milestone.

2024
Asia-Pacific Launch

Expanded to Japan, South Korea, Australia, and Singapore. 10,000th station milestone celebrated globally.

2026
12,000 Stations & Beyond

750,000+ active drivers. 48 countries. Planning IPO and expansion into Africa and South America. The mission continues.
