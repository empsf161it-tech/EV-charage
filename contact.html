Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does installation take?
A home Level 2 charger takes 2–4 hours. Commercial DC fast chargers require 1–3 days depending on grid connection requirements.
Do I need a new electrical panel?
Our certified electricians will assess your current panel during the free site survey. Most modern homes (200A+) don't need an upgrade for Level 2 charging.
What EVs are compatible?
VoltGrid chargers support all EVs sold in North America and Europe, including CCS2, CHAdeMO, NACS (Tesla), and Type 2 AC connectors.
Is there a monthly fee for public charging?
No subscription needed for PAYG access. VoltPass at $19/month gives a 30% discount on every kWh plus priority bay access.
